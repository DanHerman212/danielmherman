{% comment %} The conversation: one continuous column of turns, oldest first. This is the only zone that scrolls, and the turns are constrained to a centred column so they do not run flush against the sidebar. There is no navigator above the composer and no card to fold away. Each turn's question heads it and sticks to the top of the scroll area while its own answer passes under it, so the reader always has the question that the text in front of them is answering — which is what the navigator was for, without a second control to keep in step with the first. The blocks are built by the client and nothing in them is addressed by a page-wide id, because a conversation holds several canvases, several citation triggers and several status rows at once. `data-renderer-url` names the A2UI bundle for the feed as a whole: it is fetched on demand, once, and mounted per turn — never at page load, because its module graph is large and nothing on first paint needs it. {% endcomment %}
